howdoisound

Nine pads, three seconds each. Record through the camera, pull audio out of any video in your camera roll, or import a voice memo. Trim on the waveform, then play.

Looper. Tap Looper to arm, hit pads to lay down a pattern, tap again to close the loop. Anything you play after that gets overdubbed. Tap a third time to clear.

Per pad. Edit mode gives you level, pitch from half to double speed, reverse, and sustained loop.

Hosting this yourself. This is one self contained HTML file with no dependencies. Drop it on any static host and it runs as howdoisound. Served from your own domain instead of an embedded frame, the browser will grant the microphone directly, so Rec mode records in place with no camera detour.
